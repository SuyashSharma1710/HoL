### CELL WITH TOXINS vs DETOXED CELL

#### CELL WITH TOXINS
*Toxic overload weakens cell function and drains your energy.*

- **TOXIN BUILD-UP:** Chemicals, heavy metals and free radicals accumulate in the cell.
- **LOW ENERGY (MITOCHONDRIA):** Mitochondria are damaged, producing less ATP.
- **OXIDATIVE STRESS:** Causes inflammation and damages cell components.
- **WASTE ACCUMULATION:** Cellular waste builds up, blocking normal function.
- **WEAK CELL DEFENSE:** Cell becomes vulnerable to disease and degeneration.

**RESULT:**
- Low Energy
- Fatigue
- Weak Immunity
- Inflammation
- Premature Aging

> **When cells are toxic, you feel tired, heavy and sick.**

---

#### DETOXED CELL
*Detoxification cleanses cells and restores energy, repair and vitality.*

- **TOXIN REMOVAL:** Toxins and heavy metals are flushed out of the cell.
- **HIGH ENERGY (MITOCHONDRIA):** Healthy mitochondria produce abundant ATP.
- **REDUCED OXIDATIVE STRESS:** Cell is protected from damage and inflammation.
- **EFFICIENT WASTE REMOVAL:** Cellular waste is cleared, supporting optimal function.
- **STRONG CELL DEFENSE:** Cell is resilient, protected and ready to heal.

**RESULT:**
- High Energy
- Better Mood
- Strong Immunity
- Healthy Aging
- Longevity

> **When cells are clean, you feel light, active and alive.**
