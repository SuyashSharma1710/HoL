# Relume AI Prompt: Harmony of Life

Copy and paste the prompt below into Relume AI to generate the sitemap and wireframes for the Harmony of Life website.

---

**Context & Brand Identity:**
You are generating a sitemap and landing page wireframes for a premium holistic wellness brand called "Harmony of Life." The brand is built on the scientific and spiritual premise that true health, youthfulness, and longevity ("Healthspan") start at the cellular level. Driven by the core tagline **"Live Young"**, they believe that by increasing "Cellular Charge" (cellular voltage) through nutrition, detoxification, and lifestyle changes, individuals can elevate their overall "Lifeforce" and reverse biological aging. The brand offers a personalized supplement product funnel (Foundation, Insurance, Longevity), lifestyle education, and a community ecosystem led by Dr. Ashutosh Rastogi.

**Design Aesthetic & Tone:**
The visual style is a blend of advanced microscopic science and ancient holistic spirituality. The design should feel premium, transformative, and empowering.

**Goal:**
Please generate a comprehensive sitemap and a detailed landing page wireframe that logically flows through the 9 core sections provided below. The goal of the page is to educate the user on the problem (declining cellular voltage/lifeforce due to modern lifestyles), present the solution (the 12 Foundational Pillars & personalized products), and convert them into joining the community or starting their health journey.

**Required Landing Page Sections / Flow:**

**1. Hero Section (Welcome to Harmony of Life)**
- Headline: Live Young. Increase Your Cellular Charge. Elevate Your Lifeforce. Transform Your Life.
- Subheadline: Every heartbeat and breath begins within your cells. Support healthy cellular function to flourish.
- Primary CTA: Start Your Journey

**2. The Problem (Why Harmony of Life Exists)**
- Concept: You are living longer, but are you living healthier?
- Focus on "Healthspan" vs. Lifespan.

**3. The Science (Cellular Charge & You are Electric)**
- Explain that lifeforce manifests as cellular charge.
- Key stats: The body has 50 Trillion cells. Healthy membrane potential is 70mV.

**4. The Inhibitors (What Reduces Lifeforce?)**
- Highlight modern lifestyle problems: toxins, stress, lack of sleep, poor nutrition, and how they cause a drop in cellular voltage and lead to aging.

**5. The Solution (The 12 Foundational Pillars)**
- A visual section (grid or circular layout) showcasing the 12 pillars (Balance Nutrition, Deep Detox, Artery Cleanse, Alkaline Chemistry, Cellular Vitality, Gut Reset, Inflammation, Deep Sleep, Regular Exercise and Yoga, Immunity, Social Connect, Nature Connect) plus the core practice of Meditation/Dhyan.

**6. Authority / Trust (Meet Dr. Rastogi)**
- Section to introduce Dr. Ashutosh Rastogi, his vision, and professional background.

**7. Community Ecosystem (Harmony Community)**
- Highlight the support system: WhatsApp Support, Doctors & Nutritionists, Challenges, Retreats.
- Must include a sub-section for Testimonials and Social Proof.

**8. The Program (Living Young)**
- Overview of the specific program focused on decreasing biological age by supporting mitochondria and detoxification.

**9. Final Call to Action (CTA)**
- Strong final push to join the movement, consult a health expert, or purchase the foundation kit to start Living Young.
